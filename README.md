# Operations & AI Systems | Building Intelligent Automation for Scale

I architect AI-powered systems that solve structural operational problems: real-time detection systems that shift response from reactive to proactive, decision-support tools that let operators test changes before deployment, and governance systems that intercept policy drift before it becomes a customer issue. Across support operations, marketplace safety, and CX, I design workflows where automation and human judgment work together to move faster without sacrificing control.

## Featured Projects

### **[Process Gap Detector](https://github.com/dhivya90m/Gap-Detector)**
A 24/7 Slack-native policy enforcement engine. Monitors Slack conversations in real time for policy signals (proposals and deviations), routes proposals through human-in-the-loop approval, surgically updates Notion policies via Gemini-powered rule resolution, and logs every action to an audit database. Shifts policy governance from reactive discovery to real-time prevention.

**Tech:** Python, Slack Socket Mode, Notion API, Google Gemini, n8n

---

### **[LogiSight Analytics](https://github.com/dhivya90m/LogiSight-Analytics)**
A browser-based operations intelligence platform designed to compress the entire ops analytics workflow into one interface. Features real-time data ingestion, SQL-based data cleaning, AI-powered reasoning (plain English questions answered with data), what-if simulation lab for testing rules before live deployment, and stakeholder-segmented action center. Turns flat spreadsheets into structured, actionable systems.

**Tech:** TypeScript, React, SQL, Google Gemini, Notion

---

### **[Ops Pulse (Safety Anomaly Detector)](https://github.com/dhivya90m/Delivery-KPI-Monitoring-Agent)**
An autonomous safety intelligence pipeline that acts as a 24/7 analyst. Ingests live incident data, runs trend and root-cause analysis, filters for true P0 crises, and fires executive alerts to Slack within minutes — not weeks. Shifted marketplace safety response from weekly reviews to same-hour intervention.

**Tech:** n8n, Google Gemini, Notion, Slack API

---

### **[Salesforce SLA Risk Analyzer](https://github.com/dhivya90m/On-time-delivery-AI-agent)**
A predictive agent that monitors ticket age and complexity in real time, predicts near-breach risk and CSAT outcomes, and auto-routes escalations before SLA failure. Replaced reactive weekly SLA reports with proactive same-day intervention.

**Tech:** Salesforce Agentforce, Einstein AI, Salesforce Flow
